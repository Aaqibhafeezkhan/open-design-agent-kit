## T1 flaws (from the review shots)

1. **The glass reads as milky grey**, not clear: the lilac background doesn't show through it, and everything inside is washed out. It's `transmission` glass rendered in software WebGL; the skill doesn't warn about it.
2. **The lake and snow are far too large.** The brief asked for a *small* lake and a *patch* of snow; the lake covers about a quarter of the planet, and the snow covers the whole top.
3. **The props don't read.** At the overview distance, the houses are red dots and trees on the silhouette look like thorns. The lighthouse sticks out sideways like a cigarette.
4. **Framing:** the object fills about 35% of the frame height, and the rest is empty lilac (78% of pixels are one color).

## T2 flaws

1. **No chessboard.** The gold border was modeled as a solid slab over the whole board, so the squares and the rune border never show. The check passed.
2. **The knights are slabs**: an extruded profile that doesn't read as a horse head from most angles.
3. **The table is a hexagonal plinth** smaller than the board, so the board seems to float (low-angle shot).
4. **The hall is one wall of arches**; the other sides are black. The floor reads as bright bathroom tile, not stone.
5. **The skill's stage rule** ("a ground plane, nothing else") contradicts the brief's hall. The agent had to choose without guidance.

## Things the review couldn't do

- **Wireframe shot:** impossible. The scene isn't reachable from the page or the console (`window.odScene` exposes only `ready`, `rotate` and `rotationSeconds`).
- **Low angle:** reachable only by dragging, and limited by `maxPolarAngle`. There's no repeatable named view.
- **Render budget:** triangle and draw-call counts aren't exposed, so none were reported.
