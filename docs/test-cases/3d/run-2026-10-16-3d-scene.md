## What the new skill fixed

- **T2's knights, unfixed across three earlier rounds, were solved by an asset.** The tool found Poly Haven's CC0 `chess_set`. The builder used only its carved knight geometry, in the set's own ivory and stone, and form and fidelity went from 1 to 2 in one round. This is the change's main bet working: the loop finds the flaw, and the asset source provides a fix that code alone couldn't.
- **T2's hall became real stone** from Poly Haven textures, in place of the hand-drawn canvas blocks.
- **T4 was built from five Poly Haven assets:** a laptop, a desk lamp, a succulent, stationery and a wood surface, plus a procedural mug and track. By round 3 it had no automatic failures, and its scale was right: a 4.5 cm road at 1:87 next to real-size objects.
- **The scene spec kept the layout honest.** Moving the mug and pot in the spec moved them in the render, and the critic's clearance fixes were edits to the spec.

## Findings that changed the change or the kit

- **A stage wall blocked a review view.** T2's new side walls stood between the low-view camera and the set. The check now hides stage meshes that block the review camera, for that view only. It's in the spec, with a test that covers both cases.
- **Asset search matched inside words.** "mug" matched the `smugglers_cove` collection on a fern and a ship. Matching is now on whole words, with plurals and longer stems allowed.
- **Polygons are not triangles.** Two 70k-polygon plants (about 280k triangles) took the example over budget. The tool now reports each model's polycount, with a "triangles can be up to twice this" note, and the skill says so too.
- **Poly Haven glTF textures need a URL map** (found while building the example, recorded in the design).

## What the loop still can't reach in three rounds

Materials, light and composition stayed at 1 in both cases:
- **T2:** plastic-looking ivory, a flat hall light, a weak mobile frame, and cone flames.
- **T4:** saturated wood, an unlit lamp head, and the framing.

These are craft passes: grain and bump maps, a lamp-driven light design, flame sprites, framing per aspect ratio. Each needs more than one fix per round. The critic's requests also drift between rounds; T2's black-stone clearcoat was "less" in one round and "more" in the next. Possible next steps:
- a progress gallery and a Tweaks-style light panel (the Argon guide's workflow 4), so a person steers light and materials directly;
- Nano Banana-style target renders (workflow 5), once an image tool is available.
